About Us

Australian Owned & Manufactured Haircare Products since 1995

Kusco-Murphy combines years of expertise in the haircare industry along with a passion for creating haircare products that nourish, protect, and enhance the natural beauty of your hair.

Additionally our brand was founded in 1995 by two of Melbourne's most prestigious hair stylists. Not to mention that we remain based in Melbourne, Victoria, Australia.

Dedicated to sustainability and ethical practices

We work with local suppliers and manufacturers to reduce their carbon footprint as well as supporting initiatives that promote environmental conservation and social responsibility.

Our product range contains no paper or animal by-products and we emphatically will never test our products on animals.

Furthermore to contribute to a sustainable environment Kusco-Murphy uses recycled and recyclable materials for our packaging and cartons.

Kusco-Murphy are known for our premium haircare products

From shampoos and conditioners to styling products and treatments.

A standout feature of our products is their use of natural and sustainably sourced ingredients, simultaneously with a focus on using high quality ingredients and innovative formulas.

Our products deliver exceptional results

Kusco-Murphy uses botanical extracts, essential oils, and plant-based proteins that are gentle on the hair and scalp while delivering exceptional results. For dry, damaged hair in need of hydration or fine, or limp hair in need of volume, we have a product to suit your needs.

Whether you're looking to maintain your hair's health and shine, or experiment with new styles and looks. Kusco-Murphy has the products you need to achieve beautiful, healthy hair.

Experience the difference with Kusco-Murphy

In conclusion, discover why our renowned brand and products are a favorite among haircare professionals and enthusiasts around the globe. Additionally with a reputation for delivering salon quality results at home.
